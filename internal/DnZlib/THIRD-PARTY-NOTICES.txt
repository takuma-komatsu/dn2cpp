DnZlib is a Pure C# reimplementation of zlib. It is a port of zlib 1.3.2 and
additionally adopts specific performance techniques from zlib-ng and fast_zlib,
reworked as architecture-generic C# rather than copied verbatim. The upstream
copyright notices and license terms below are retained in accordance with those
licenses. This file (or an equivalent attribution) SHOULD accompany any
distribution of DnZlib or of a product that embeds it.

Summary of components:

  Component   Version   License        Binary-form attribution
  ---------   -------   -------------  ------------------------------------------
  zlib        1.3.2     zlib License   not required by the license (best practice)
  zlib-ng     2.2.5     zlib License   not required by the license (best practice)
  fast_zlib   —         BSD-3-Clause   REQUIRED — reproduce the notice below

Note: the zlib License restricts its notice requirement to *source*
distributions, so a binary-only (compiled) product is not strictly obligated to
reproduce the zlib / zlib-ng notices. They are included here as good practice
and to satisfy the "do not misrepresent the origin" clause. The fast_zlib
BSD-3-Clause license DOES require the notice to be reproduced in binary-form
distributions (e.g. in an in-app "licenses" / credits screen or accompanying
documentation).
